      Playlist settings

      Play on YouTube now needs nothing: it opens the set as a YouTube playlist (up to 50 videos per part). Saving into your own account needs a one-time, free app ID from YouTube or Spotify, because only you can sign in to your account. The IDs and sign-ins stay in this browser.

      Signing in needs the page served at http://127.0.0.1:8613/. Run python -m http.server 8613 --bind 127.0.0.1 in this folder and open that address. Spotify doesn't accept file:// or localhost addresses.

      2. Set up the OAuth consent screen (External, Testing) and add your Google account as a test user.
